# Roadmap

Planned and potential improvements for Cloud Instance Recommender. This is a
living document: items here are candidates, not commitments, and priorities
shift as the tool evolves. For what has already shipped, see
[CHANGELOG.md](CHANGELOG.md).

## Themes

_Priorities are still being set — the lists below are directions, not a
committed backlog._

- **Data & accuracy** — keeping instance data fresh and trustworthy, and making
  its provenance and freshness clear.
- **Input & ingestion** — supporting more inventory export formats and catching
  bad input early.
- **Recommendations** — deeper right-sizing and richer cross-cloud comparison.
- **Reporting & UX** — clearer results, visual summaries, and better exports.
- **Quality & security** — broader automated testing, accessibility, and
  hardening.

## Suggesting an item

Ideas are welcome — open an issue or a discussion (see
[CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md)). Concrete, self-contained proposals are the
easiest to pick up.
